The purpose of this assignment is networking, and learn about how Statistics is used in industry. 
\begin{enumerate}
\item Contact a company (by e-mail, phone or in person) that might use Statistics, and you are interested in. Please provide the contact information. I understand that e-mail is the easiest way to contact them, but if you talk to somebody in person or on the phone it gives you a better learning experience and opportunity for networking. One possibility is to e-mail them, and explain the assignment, and ask them for an appointment with a person who actually can answer your questions. Try to talk to a person who uses Statistics on a daily bases (or at least sometimes).
\item Provide general information about the company. What do they do? Where are they located? How many employees do they have? Etc.
\item Ask your contact person the following, and report the answers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item What is her/his role in the company?
\item How does he/she uses Statistics in his/her role?
\item What type of projects does the company do that require some use of statistical tools.
\item What statistical software do people use in the company?
\end{enumerate}
\item Come up with $5$ questions you want to ask them about the job, about the company, about the use of Statistics within the company etc. Ask those questions, and report the answers. 
\end{enumerate}
