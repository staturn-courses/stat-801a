
\item \textbf{Corn.} Yield of corn (bushels per acre) in Nebraska counties in 2009 can be described by a Normal distribution with a mean of 178 bushels per acre and a standard deviation of 16 bushels per acre. Use \textbf{the 68-95-99.7 Rule (Empirical Rule)} to answer the following questions. (Hint: Draw pictures of the normal curve.)
\begin{enumerate}
\item The middle $68\%$ of counties have a corn yield \textbf{between} what two values?
\item What is the value of the $84^{th}$ percentile of corn yield for counties in Iowa?  
\item What proportion of counties have a corn yield \textbf{between} 162 and 226 bushels per acre?
\item $0.15\%$ of counties have a corn yield \textbf{less than} or equal to what value?
\item  What proportion of counties have a corn yield of \textbf{at least} 210 bushels per acre?
\end{enumerate}
