
\item \textbf{Notation.} Suppose that a random variable, $X$, follows a Normal distribution. Specifically, $X \sim \mathcal{N}(-2, 16)$. 
\begin{enumerate}
\item What is the value of the mean?
\item What is the value of the median?
\item What is the value of the mode?
\item What is the value of the standard deviation?
\item What is the value of the variance?
\item What is the total area under the density curve?
\item Multiple Choice.  What is the range of values the random variable, X, can take on?  (Enter the roman numeral corresponding to the correct answer, i.e. enter i, ii, iii, iv, or v.)
\begin{enumerate}
\item -14 to 10
\item $-\infty$ to $\infty$
\item -50 to 46
\item -26 to 22
\item -98 to 94
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
