Researchers are interested in investigating whether exposure to different volatile compounds influences the growth of southern magnolia (Magnolia virginiana) seedlings. Previous research on volatile compounds and ambrosia beetles has motivated interest in understanding how these compounds interact with plants.

Researchers conduct an experiment with a completely randomized design (CRD) in which seedlings are randomly assigned to one of five treatments:

  • Control (no volatile exposure)

  • Acetaldehyde

  • Acetone

  • Ethanol

  • Methanol

There were 50 seeds available for the experiment, and each treatment randomly received 10 seeds. After a specified exposure period, researchers measure the root length (cm) of each seedling.

Part 1
