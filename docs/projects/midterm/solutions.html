Question 10

  1. t = 3.9831 is the test statistic, obtained from \(\frac{\bar{X}-\mu}{s/\sqrt{n}}\).
  2. df = 36 is the degrees of freedom, obtained by 37 - 1 (only 37 complete observations)
  3. p-value = 0.0001584 is the probability of observing values of \(\bar{X}_{iCBT, T0}-\bar{X}_{iCBT, T2}\) that are as or more extreme than our observed statistic assuming \(H_0\) is true. Obtained with the pt() function.
  4. (okay if not present in solution) alternative hypothesis: true mean difference is greater than 13: statement of the alternative hypothesis.
  5. 95 percent confidence interval: (20.64541, Inf) is the one-sided confidence interval for \(\mu\).
  6. mean difference: 26.27027 is the observed difference in means.

Extra Credit

Each hypothesis test has a probability of making a Type I error (with probability \(\alpha\)). Performing multiple tests increases the probability of making at least one Type I error across the collection of tests. This is known as multiplicity, which affects the collective error rate across all of the tests.
